Centre of mass & support contacts

The nervous system combines information from vision, the inner ear, and receptors in muscles and joints to estimate body position and motion. Muscles act on that information to help maintain or change posture.

Try the controls while seated or using your usual support. Select a support example, move the projection slider, then change its width and observe when the projected point crosses a boundary. For example, changing the contact region changes where that boundary falls in the diagram. Read about the senses involved in balance.

Click the diagram or use the projection slider to explore an illustrative projection onto the ground. This is a simplified geometry model, not a measurement of a person or a prediction of balance or falling.

Contact points, support forces and pressure are schematic. Seat support, foot placement, wheel contacts, brakes, muscle responses, friction and acceleration are not calculated.
