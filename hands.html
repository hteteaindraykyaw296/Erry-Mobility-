Your options

Choose your preferred hand, both hands or another access method. Children and young people can explore with a trusted adult or care team when support is helpful. The person’s comfort, communication and choices guide the activity.

How spoon shape and position change the task
  1. Compare the handle shapes in the drawing.
  2. Notice how handle width or angle could change the grip or wrist movement.
  3. Consider how a shorter reach to the utensil might change the task.

For example, a wider grip may suit someone who prefers less finger closure. Added bulk or weight can make the same tool harder to use for someone else. The demonstration covers utensil handling. Eating and swallowing need individual guidance.
