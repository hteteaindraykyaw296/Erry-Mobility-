Autonomy, joy and pride in being disabled are central to this project. Choose goals that matter to you, including creative expression, communication, comfortable participation or rest.

  1. Advance informed awareness

    Present clear, evidence-informed information about CP, its causes, varied experiences, care approaches and available support, with identifiable sources.

  2. Affirm dignity and self-acceptance

    Use respectful, encouraging language that embraces disability, recognises emotional wellbeing and supports personal agency, belonging and hope.

  3. Support everyday participation

    Offer accessible learning tools and examples of adaptations for movement, typing, writing and daily tasks, with comfort and individual priorities guiding exploration.

  4. Enable personal reflection

    Provide an optional journal for experiences, mood, chosen support and personally meaningful observations. Treat body measurements as optional context and respect each person’s own understanding of progress.

  5. Apply a biomedical engineering perspective

    Connect lived needs with anatomy, motor control, biomechanics, human factors and assistive design to develop clear questions that can be investigated responsibly.

  6. Pursue inclusive development and evaluation

    Seek future collaboration with people with CP, therapists, clinicians and engineers to review accessibility, affordability, safety and usefulness through appropriate supervised evaluation.
